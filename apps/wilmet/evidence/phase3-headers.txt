Compared against the plan's checklist (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options,
X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy):

- X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, HSTS: already implemented,
  confirmed present.
- CSP: implemented but deliberately report-only, per an explicit comment
  in the source explaining the team is staging it — waiting to observe
  real traffic on a working deployment before enforcing. Reasonable,
  intentional, not an oversight. Revisit once the site is live again.
- X-Frame-Options: genuinely missing. No `frame-ancestors` CSP directive
  either, so there is currently NO clickjacking protection active at
  all. This is the one real, actionable gap. Queued as a Lovable prompt
  per Salma's choice (see the two prepared prompts for when credits
  return).
